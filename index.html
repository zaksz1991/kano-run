QUICK GUIDE

Drive Kano streets

  • LEFT / RIGHT — change lane. Swipe on the road or use arrow keys / A-D.
  • GAS — accelerate. BRAKE — slow down to board and drop passengers.
  • GREEN PAD — passenger pickup. GOLD PAD — passenger drop-off.
    Stop almost completely first.
  • FUEL — stop on a fuel pad. Potholes damage tyres and vehicle condition.
  • KAROTA — pay, talk, or run. Escaping raises heat.
  • SPACE — brake; H — horn; ESC — menu.
