WHAT STEP 110 CHANGES
- Builds the active life-rig list only when renderer.lifeScene changes.
- On the road, no bedroom, household, breakfast, garage, or other inactive life-stage rigs receive procedural animation updates.
- When a life scene becomes active, only its own rigs are made visible and animated.
- Avoids redundant market-root visibility assignments.
- Skips empty ambient-crowd and walking-passenger update calls.
- Leaves road traffic, collision, player physics, passenger/economy state, and active life-scene animation rules unchanged.
