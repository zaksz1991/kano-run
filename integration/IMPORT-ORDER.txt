TASK 29 — TIME-BASED JUNCTION TURN CONTROLLER

1. Copy js/KanoJunctionTurnRules.js and js/KanoJunctionTurnRuntime.js into the existing js/ folder.
2. Add `import './KanoJunctionTurnRuntime.js';` to the existing activation bridge after its shared imports and before the module that dynamically imports main.js.
3. To animate a vehicle, the real traffic loop must dispatch `kano:traffic-turn-start` with a vehicle Object3D and actual start/end positions in scene coordinates, plus `turn: 'left' | 'straight' | 'right'`. Optional `durationMs` defaults to 1400 and is clamped to 250–10000 ms.
4. To cancel a vehicle's current turn, dispatch `kano:traffic-turn-cancel` with `{ vehicle }`.
5. Do not dispatch turn-start every frame. Dispatch once when the vehicle enters a junction; the runtime animates the movement itself.
6. Preserve all existing imports and do not replace the Claude activation bridge wholesale. The actual traffic loop and scene-space junction endpoints are not present in this package, so this import alone does not activate turns for existing traffic.
